The thread prefix IDs that mean a clerk has taken an application on. A queue thread carrying any of them is being worked and is never reminded, whoever put the prefix there and whether or not they still hold the seat. Do not list the enlistment type prefixes (57, 58) here: every application in the queue carries one, so listing them would stop the reminder firing at all. Enter one ID per line or separate with commas. Default: 53, 54, 55 (Hold, Approved, In Progress).
